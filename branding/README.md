# QuickDrop identity

Original mark: a white paired-arrow/ribbon form suggesting a fast Q on royal blue.
The master is `quickdrop-icon.png`; it was generated specifically for this project.
Native icon slots are packaged at their original required dimensions by
`support/quickdrop/brand.py` (Python + Pillow). The resulting resources are included,
so building the app does not require Pillow or an image-generation service.

Palette: QuickDrop blue #1769FF, deep blue #164BC0, paper #F5F8FF,
night #091426, white #FFFFFF. Platform typography remains native/system-based,
including the original CJK font fallbacks. No proprietary fonts were added.

Onboarding illustrations are live Flutter compositions in
`app/lib/quickdrop/onboarding_page.dart`, with accessible text scaling and reduced-motion handling.
This identity is a design deliverable, not a trademark-registration or clearance claim.
